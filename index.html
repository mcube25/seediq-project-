Key Quality Factors

Understanding the critical parameters that determine castoria seed quality and value.

Weight Density

Higher weight indicates better oil content and seed maturity. Premium seeds have 14-16g per 100 seeds.

Size Uniformity

Consistent seed size ensures even processing and higher oil extraction efficiency during production.

Color Consistency

Uniform dark brown color indicates proper maturity and optimal harvesting time for maximum quality.

Germination Rate

Directly correlated with seed viability. Premium seeds achieve 95%+ germination under standard conditions.
