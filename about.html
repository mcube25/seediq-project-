About This Project

The Castoria Seed Quality Analyzer is an advanced tool designed to help farmers, traders, and agricultural researchers quickly and accurately grade castoria seeds according to international standards.

Using machine learning and computer vision technology, this tool analyzes seed images to determine size, estimate weight, and classify seeds into appropriate quality grades.

Our grading system is based on the International Castoria Seed Standards (ICSS) and has been validated through extensive research and field testing across multiple growing regions.
